## Field policy and academic accuracy

- The visible paper reference is the existing permanent `ICH27-P...` value; **never** use the DB numeric ID as a public reference.
- All ordered authors and the title/track are immutable per-issue snapshots. The template displays a single LoA per Published Accepted paper, not a separate document per coauthor.
- The actual reviewer workflow uses **one** exclusive reviewer with Accept/Reject only, followed by formal academic decision/bulk publish. Do **not** claim `double-blind review`, `a panel of multiple reviewers`, `manuscript accepted`, `guaranteed indexing`, or `full-paper publication` unless independently supported by an approved policy.
- No presentation mode row until a definitive mode exists; no publication certificate, finance Verified, or presentation schedule implication.
- No author email addresses or private file links in the public QR verification result.
- Date and Roman month + calendar year must be consistent; 15 December 2026 is `XII/2026`, January 2027 is `I/2027`.
- Sample content in the visual PDF (including number, date, title, authors and track) is **illustrative, NOT an actual issued LoA**. Verification QR and signature are placeholders only.
